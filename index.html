Freedio

Turn text into sound.

Freedio reads your books to you in a warm, natural voice, entirely on your iPhone. Bring your own book, press play, and listen. No account, no subscription, no ads, and nothing ever leaves your phone.

What it does

  • Reads EPUB, PDF, and text files you add from Files or the share sheet
  • A warm neural voice that runs fully on your device
  • Works completely offline
  • Several voices and adjustable reading speed
  • Follow along with the text and tap any line to jump there
  • Bookmarks, chapter list, sleep timer, lock screen and AirPods controls
  • Remembers your place in every book

Privacy Policy

Freedio collects no data. The app runs entirely on your device and makes no network connections. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no ads. The books you add and your reading progress stay on your device. Because Freedio collects nothing, there is nothing for us to store, share, sell, or delete.

Freedio offers optional, one-time tips through Apple's in-app purchase system. These payments are processed entirely by Apple under Apple's terms; Freedio never sees or stores your name, card, or any payment details.

If iOS or Apple's frameworks collect diagnostics at the system level, that is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not Freedio. Last updated May 2026.
